# Selora Beauty — Ad Creative Bible (TikTok/Snap, Saudi market)

**Products:** Stretch-Mark Serum (خطوة ١ — يتغلغل ويطمس التشققات الملونة) + Stretch-Mark Cream (خطوة ٢ — حاجز زبدي يمنع تشققات جديدة). Offer to push: الروتين الكامل 279 ر.س (وفّري ١١٩). COD. Ships KSA.

**Audience:** Saudi women 20–45. Core emotional wound: postpartum/pregnancy stretch marks on belly, thighs, hips, chest — hiding from mirrors, avoiding fitted clothes, grief for "pre-baby body," shame nobody talks about.

**Golden rules 2026:**
- Hook in first 1.5s or you're dead. Native/iPhone look > polished studio.
- 15–30s sweet spot. 9:16 always. Arabic captions ON every video (85% watch muted).
- Female Saudi-dialect VO, conversational, zero ad-voice.
- Never say "إزالة/شفاء نهائي" — use يخفّف / يفتّح / يحسّن مظهر (compliance + believability).
- Always end on the DUO: "السيروم لحاله يشتغل، بس مع الكريم النتيجة تتضاعف — اطلبي الروتين الكامل، الدفع عند الاستلام."
- Every video = one angle. Don't mix. Test 5, kill 3, scale 1–2.

**Structure:**

## VIDEO 5 — Red vs White urgency "لا تستنين"

- 9–16s: "كل أسبوع تأخير صار أصعب" visual timeline (red→white fade graphic)
- 16–25s: serum targets the colored phase → CTA urgency.

**Voiceover:**

## Sound design
- Videos 1, 6, 8: soft emotional ambient (oud/piano, low volume under VO)

- Videos 3, 10: subtle modern corporate tick sound on text pops
- Videos 4, 9: trending Saudi/Gulf audio — check TikTok Creative Center KW weekly

## Avatar generation (Nano Banana Pro)
1. Generate base portrait: `Saudi female dermatologist, white coat, hijab, warm clinic, direct eye contact, photorealistic, 9:16`
2. Feed to lip-sync tool (HeyGen/D-ID/whatever you use) with the VO script.
3. Kill the "AI look": slight handheld crop animation + film grain overlay + real clinic ambience.

## Sora/Veo prompt template (steal this structure)
`9:16 vertical, [shot type: macro/handheld iPhone/cinematic], [subject + action], [location], [lighting], [camera movement], photorealistic, natural skin texture, [mood], no text, no watermark`

Always append: `photorealistic, natural skin texture, no text`. Generate each shot separately, cut in CapCut.

## Compliance guardrails (don't get the ad account flagged)
- No "يزيل نهائياً" — always "يفتّح/يخفّف/يحسّن مظهر"
- No extreme graphic skin close-ups in the first frame (TikTok review)
- Before/after: keep believable (faded not erased)
- No claims during breastfeeding unless worded "آمن خلال الحمل" (already on site)
- COD emphasis is allowed and converts — keep it

## Testing plan
- Launch order: 1 (grief), 3 (authority), 4 (skeptic) — these cover emotion/logic/objection
- 48–72h kill rule: hook rate <25% (3s view) or CTR <1% → kill
- Winner → make 3 hook variants of it
- Video 10 retargets engagers/viewers — never cold
- Snap: reuse everything, cut to 6–15s versions
